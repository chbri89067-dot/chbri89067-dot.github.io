EARTH → SPACE / PHONE TEST

Put the cockpit in your viewer.

Before putting the phone in the viewer, turn it sideways and tap Start to allow motion and sound. After setup, use your head to aim the center reticle; the experience uses hands-free gaze selection.

  1. Look at the purple LAUNCH control for 1.8 seconds.
  2. In space, look at GRAVITY to float or settle down. After Scene 2, select CONTINUE TO MARS.
  3. Look at LAND to descend. On Mars, select EXPLORE, then look at the glowing habitat markers.
  4. Look behind you for cabin movement, height, sound and exit controls. Look away before selecting the same control again.

Hold the center reticle on a control until the purple ring fills. Screen taps do not select controls while the viewer is running. Selection starts after a short settling delay. This tracks head turning, not your eyes or physical walking.
