Mobile

  1. Open the mobile link in Safari and turn the phone sideways.
  2. Tap Allow motion & start viewer, approve motion and move the phone slightly before putting it in the headset.
  3. Aim with your head and complete the normal gaze hold. The ring fills clockwise and selects once. Ordinary controls still require looking away before another selection.
  4. In Scene 3, a subtle hairline mark near the upper-right window edge hides the Easter egg. One completed gaze there plays all four cracks automatically, then the glass breaks and the original float-out begins. No break button or repeated look-away cycle is needed.

Fast mobile glass test. Scene 3 waits for setup so permission time does not use up the cruise.

Computer

Drag with the mouse to look, click to select, and use WASD to move. G changes gravity. The original computer window Easter egg still uses four clicks on the forward glass. No phone setup is shown.

Both versions preserve the clear final Mars scene. The phone viewer tracks head rotation, not physical forward/backward walking. Computer keyboard movement remains.
