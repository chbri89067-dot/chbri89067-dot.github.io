  1. Open a test link in Safari, turn the phone sideways, choose your selection hold (default 1 second), then tap Allow motion & start viewer.
  2. Approve motion and move the phone slightly before putting it into your headset.
  3. Aim the center reticle with your head. A ring appears and fills clockwise, then selects once. Looking away cancels or allows a fresh selection.
  4. Gaze at LAUNCH, use GRAVITY, and let the cruise and Mars approach run. Then gaze at LAND. The final Mars view opens automatically.
  5. In Scenes 1–5, select Menu for the existing controls or Hide Menu to collapse them. The final scene removes navigation buttons and their containers for a clear Mars view.
  6. In Scene 3, gaze at the forward window four separate times, looking away between selections, to trigger Jesse’s optional window break and float-out.

Screen taps do not select buttons in viewer mode. The setup gesture is required for Safari motion and audio. In Scenes 1–5, open Menu and gaze at Exit Viewer to exit. The final scene has no navigation menu.
