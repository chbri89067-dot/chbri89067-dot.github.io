  1. Turn off Portrait Orientation Lock and rotate the phone sideways.
  2. Tap Allow motion & start viewer, allow motion, and gently turn the phone.
  3. Place the phone in your Cardboard-style viewer.
  4. Hold your gaze on the purple LAUNCH button for 1.8 seconds.
  5. In space, gaze at GRAVITY to float. Look away and back to restore it.

Turn around for movement, height, sound and exit controls. After exiting, use Record my phone test to save the real-device results.

The ZIP is the editable project. Opening index.html directly from the iPhone Files app does not provide the HTTPS needed for motion sensors; use the phone-test link for the headset test. No ChatGPT login is needed.
