Download the integrated project ZIP

  1. Open a test link in Safari and rotate the phone sideways.
  2. Before putting it in the viewer, tap Allow motion & start viewer and approve motion access.
  3. Use head-gaze dwell: aim the center reticle at a control for 1.8 seconds until the purple ring fills.
  4. After Scene 2, select CONTINUE TO MARS, then LAND.
  5. After landing, select EXPLORE and look at the Alpha, Beta and Gamma habitat markers.

Look behind you for movement, sound, recenter and exit controls. Screen taps do not select in-world controls during headset mode.

No account is needed. The physical iPhone/headset combination still needs your test. The generic stereo profile has no viewer QR calibration or lens correction.

To run the extracted project on a computer, use Node.js and Start-VR.cmd or npm start. Use an HTTPS website for the iPhone sensor test; opening index.html from Files is insufficient.
