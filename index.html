01 / DATABASE SYSTEMSTEAM PROJECT

HoopBase

From shot logs to basketball insights.

PythonpandasMySQLFlask

A database-backed NBA explorer with player comparisons, team histories, leaderboards, and interactive shot charts.

Behind the project

The question

How can individual shot events become useful player and team statistics?

The approach

The team project normalizes CSV columns, loads relational tables in batches, and calculates field-goal percentages and points through SQL views. Flask connects those results to the interface.

My contribution

I contributed project files and frontend templates to the main branch, reorganized the application, and documented the dataset setup. My later frontend/backend proposal remains open as PR #1.

Engineering next step

Make repeat imports safe at the shot level and add data-quality checks. The current loader ignores duplicate games but can still insert duplicate shots.

02 / GEOSPATIAL ANALYSISTEAM PROJECT

Where stores meet communities

Dollar General density in North Carolina.

D3.jsGeoJSONCensus data

An interactive exploration of store density and poverty, comparing places with the surrounding county.

Behind the project

The question

How does Dollar General store density vary across North Carolina communities, and how does it relate to poverty?

The approach

The team visualization combines geographic boundaries, store locations, and census measures. Maps, comparisons, and a scatterplot help readers explore the relationships.

My role

Collaborative CSC 362 final project with Patrick Grimes. The README credits Patrick and Brian as authors. Visible commits are attributed to Patrick, so individual implementation ownership is not claimed here.

Analytical care

Place and county are different units. Store density and poverty describe an association; they do not establish a causal relationship.
